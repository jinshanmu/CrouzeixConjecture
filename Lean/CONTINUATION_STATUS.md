## Active v4 proof route

1. The finite complex Euclidean model, numerical range, functional calculus,
   positivity, spectrum, generated algebra, compactness, spectral inclusion,
   and Toeplitz--Hausdorff convexity are proved.
2. For the positive-real completion theorem, the auxiliary matrix `B` has a
   `SimpleDiagonalization`, while the target has the form
   `T = S diag(lambda) S⁻¹` in the same basis. Only
   `‖lambda i‖ ≤ 1` is assumed; repetitions and zeros are allowed.
3. The defect condition is relative to `alg(Bᴴ)`. Pullback produces the model
   `G(I-zΛ)⁻¹ + D(z)G`. The kernel is sampled at one point for each
   `lambda i` together with zero; the arbitrary-finite-type sampling theorem
   does not require these points to be distinct.
4. The unknown diagonal correction cancels. The resulting inequality passes
   through the two Gramians, the eigenvector argument, the first nonconstant
   Gramian term, square roots,
   and polar decomposition to prove `‖T‖ ≤ 2`. In manuscript notation the
   three matrices are `P,Q,Y`; Lean calls them
   `completionP, completionR, completionX`.
5. Compactness selects an open convex buffer inside the supplied open
   neighborhood. A direct scalar Cauchy formula on that buffer and its
   entrywise matrix form prove the exact power Cauchy identities for
   holomorphic `f` in the auxiliary basis.
6. Those identities feed the Cayley series. The double-layer construction
   produces a positive-real completion for `(B, f(B))`; its companion lies in
   `alg(B)`, so the defect lies in `alg(Bᴴ)`. No equality with the algebra
   generated by `f(B)` is needed.
7. `norm_functionEval_le_two_of_holomorphic_of_simpleDiagonalization` gives
   the normalized simple-spectrum estimate; its scaled wrapper handles the
   zero maximum directly and otherwise divides only by that maximum.
8. On each fixed outer domain, contour continuity passes the estimate from
   simple-spectrum `Bₖ → A` to `holomorphicMatrixEval A f`.
9. Compactness supplies a positive buffer inside the given open holomorphy
   neighborhood. A sufficiently small tail of canonical outer bodies has
   closure in that buffer; their exact maxima decrease to the maximum on
   `W(A)`, proving `holomorphicCrouzeixBound` without extending `f` globally.
10. Rational compatibility gives the finite rational corollary directly;
    subtraction and polynomial/rational compatibility give the two
    matrix-function error estimates.

No Runge theorem, Mergelyan theorem, or custom analytic axiom is assumed in
this route.
